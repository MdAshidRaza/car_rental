# 🚗 Wayfleet – Vehicle Rental Platform

A modern, responsive vehicle rental website that allows users to explore vehicles, compare prices, select rental dates, and make bookings.

Wayfleet provides a simple and user-friendly interface for renting cars, SUVs, vans, and bikes. This project is built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## 🌟 Features

- **Vehicle Listings:** Explore different vehicles, including cars, SUVs, vans, and bikes.
- **Search & Filter:** Search vehicles by city, rental dates, and vehicle type.
- **Sorting:** Sort vehicles by price, seating capacity, and recommendations.
- **Booking System:** Book vehicles by entering personal details and rental dates.
- **Price Calculation:** Automatically calculates rental charges, additional services, and taxes.
- **Additional Services:** Choose full insurance and driver services.
- **My Bookings:** View and cancel existing bookings.
- **Dark & Light Theme:** Switch between dark and light modes.
- **Responsive Design:** Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **Local Storage:** Saves bookings in the browser.

## 💡 How It Works

1. Select a pickup city and rental dates.
2. Browse available vehicles.
3. Filter or sort vehicles according to your preferences.
4. Select a vehicle and click the Book button.
5. Enter your details and choose additional services.
6. Confirm your booking.
7. View or cancel your booking in the My Bookings section.
